Spaghetti Alfredo


Love me some Spaghetti Alfredo!


Honestly, who doesn't love Spaghetti Alfredo? And when you feel the SLURP it really makes the palet moist. Don't you agree? MMMMmmmMmMMMm.


Ingredients


  • Spaghetti
  • Salt
  • Butter
  • Alfredo Sauce
  • Heavy Cream
  • Garlic
  • Parm
  • Parsley

Steps


  1. Use paper towel to dry chicken
  2. Season Chicken
  3. Cover Chicken in Corn Flakes
  4. Air fry at 400 for 12 mins, flipping chicken halfway through
  5. Dip in your favorite sauce and CRUNCH
