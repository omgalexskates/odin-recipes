Air Fried Brussel Sprouts


Love me some brussel sprouts!


Honestly, who doesn't love brussel sprouts? And when you feel the CRUNCH it really makes the palet moist. Don't you agree? MMMMmmmMmMMMm.


Ingredients


  • Brussel Sprouts
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Olive Oil

Steps


  1. Cut the brussel sprouts in half
  2. Toss in a bowl with the salt pepper and olive oil
  3. Use a wax sheet and place in the air frier with the brussel sprouts
  4. Air fry at 350 for 12 mins, flipping brussel sprouts halfway through
  5. Dip in your favorite sauce and CRUNCH
