Crunchy Fried Chicken Tenders


Love me some crunchy chicken!


Honestly, who doesn't love fried chicken? And when you feel the CRUNCH it really makes the palet moist. Don't you agree? MMMMmmmMmMMMm.


Ingredients


  • Chicken Tenders
  • Seasoning
  • Corn Flakes

Steps


  1. Use paper towel to dry chicken
  2. Season Chicken
  3. Cover Chicken in Corn Flakes
  4. Air fry at 400 for 12 mins, flipping chicken halfway through
  5. Dip in your favorite sauce and CRUNCH
